Steal cars, answer payphones ☎, watch the stars, respray on Queen St kills the heat. R restarts a finished run. WEAPONS (v0.30): on foot, hold Space/F (touch FIRE, gamepad RT) to shoot where you face — crates around downtown hold pistols, machine guns and worse; Q / WPN cycles what you own. The river DROWNS you — bridges are roads and hold. A car at zero health burns, then explodes: bail with E. Wanted heat now cools slowly and not at all while a cop can see you; at 4 stars expect roadblocks and return fire. Sell stolen cars at the CHOP SHOP $ marker; skull crates start Kill Frenzies. Touch controls: FOLLOW (the default) has NO buttons on screen — everything is a tap. ON FOOT: tap the ground to walk there · tap a CAR to walk over and get in (already next to it? you hop straight in) · tap a ☎ PAYPHONE to walk over and answer it · tap a PERSON or a COP to shoot them — hold your finger down to keep firing, and you face what you tapped. With a gun out, tapping a car from far off shoots it instead; up close it still gets you in (with fists out a car tap always means get in). IN A CAR: tap where you want to go and the car drives there and stops on the gold marker — hold and drag to move the point under your finger, tap the car itself to stop where you are, tap BEHIND the car close in and it reverses in (farther behind and it loops around). DOUBLE-TAP in a car: at speed it's the handbrake; at a standstill it gets you out. One gap, stated plainly: weapon SWITCHING has no gesture yet — crates equip what you pick up, and Q / the WPN button in other schemes / gamepad Y still cycle. The other schemes keep their buttons: REVERSE there is hold BRK at a stop — it brakes first, then backs up (keyboard S, stick pulled down, gamepad LT); the speedo shows R and the car lights its white reverse lamps while you're backing up. Stick: thumb anywhere left = the steering wheel; push up/down for gas/brake. Drag-steer: hold and drag, the car steers toward your thumb and drives itself — BRK still reverses. Haptics use the Vibration API — Android/Chrome only; iOS Safari has none. Gamepad: left stick steers, RT/LT are the pedals in a car and RT is FIRE on foot, A = E, X = handbrake, Y = switch weapon. Tilt steering: Sensitivity: TILT (v0.31): roll the phone like a steering wheel to steer — the on-screen wheel mirrors your tilt. Hold the phone how you'll play, then Calibrate: that pose becomes "straight". GAS/BRK stay on screen; on foot you still walk with the stick. iOS asks permission to use motion sensors when you tap Tilt.
