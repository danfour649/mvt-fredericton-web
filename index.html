Touch controls: Stick: thumb anywhere left = analog steer + push up/down for gas/brake. Drag-steer: hold and drag, the car steers toward your thumb and drives itself. Haptics use the Vibration API — Android/Chrome only; iOS Safari has none. Gamepad: left stick steers, RT/LT are the pedals, A = E, X/Y = handbrake.
