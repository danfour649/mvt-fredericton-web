Fredericton (Bam Bam)

The official in-game music video — the MVT Fredericton town anthem, shot entirely in-game in Fredericton, New Brunswick, in the fall.

From MVT Fredericton — Queen Street to the walking bridge, the Beaverbrook and the Legislature, a skate line, a Killarney treasure dive, a Leopard on Queen, and a fish rising up through the school.
